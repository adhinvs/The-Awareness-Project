# Welcome to The Awareness Project

The Awareness Project is an India-focused, research-based curriculum designed to help you see through Digital Risk and Manipulation. Our mission is to equip you with the psychological and technical tools needed to navigate the modern digital world safely.

## How to Navigate

Whether you are here to learn about deepfakes, financial scams, or dark patterns, the content is organized into clear sections.

:::tip[Browsing on Mobile?]
If you are viewing this on a mobile device, look for the **hamburger menu** (the three horizontal lines `☰`) at the top of your screen. Tapping it will open the sidebar where all sections and modules are sorted for easy access.

:::


The project is organized into 15 focus areas of digital awareness. Follow them in order or jump to the topic that interests you.

**A simple path through the library**  

1. **Choose a section.** Start with the risk or topic you want to understand.
2. **Explore the modules.** Read the explanation and try the interactive exercises where available.
3. **Put it into practice.** Use the practical steps and protections in each module.

:::info[India-first guidance]

The Awareness Project is built for people in India. Where reporting channels, laws, payment systems, or recovery steps differ by country, the relevant section will identify the local context. If you are elsewhere, use the principles here as a starting point and confirm the local process.

:::
