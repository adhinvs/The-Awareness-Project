The Group Chat

A "guaranteed doubling scheme" lands in a 22-person family WhatsApp group on a Tuesday. You are Priya. Decide what to do at each moment.

Step 1 of 4

What Every Choice Revealed

  • Pluralistic ignorance means everyone can privately doubt something while assuming, wrongly, that they are the only one who does.
  • The bystander effect diffuses responsibility to act across everyone in the group - which means, in practice, to no one.
  • Groupthink suppresses dissent to preserve harmony, especially with family or people you respect.
  • False content spreads roughly six times faster than accurate corrections on social platforms - group silence does nothing to slow that down.
  • Deciding in advance to act as if you are the only one who noticed is the concrete counter-move.
