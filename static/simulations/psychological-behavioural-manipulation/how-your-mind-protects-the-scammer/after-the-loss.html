After the Loss

Rahul put Rs. 85,000 into an "investment app" a colleague's cousin recommended. The app has stopped letting him withdraw. Walk through the three months that follow.

Step 1 of 4

What Every Choice Revealed

  • Denial feels protective in the moment, but it delays every action that could actually limit the damage.
  • Rationalisation turns a red flag you noticed and ignored into a story that makes the decision feel reasonable in hindsight.
  • The second shame - embarrassment about being fooled - silences people more effectively than the financial loss itself.
  • Recovery scammers specifically target the silence denial and shame create. The "quiet fix" is the second theft.
  • Naming the defence mechanism out loud, to one other person, is what interrupts the cycle.
